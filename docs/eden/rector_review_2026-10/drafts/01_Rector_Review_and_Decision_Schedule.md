## Current position

The project notes report that the public repository passed eight EDEN technical discovery checks on 2 October 2026. Metadata quality improvements are implemented locally and still require production deployment. A discovery score is not a repository certificate or a finding that policies, licences and deposited data are adequate.

UIST's Handle prefix 20.500.15029 is live and matches the local declaration. On 6 October 2026, [20.500.15029/261](https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.15029/261) resolved globally to the correct public item. Complete legacy migration and redirect coverage, new-item minting and restart resilience remain separate administrator checks; the sample establishes live item resolution.

FAIRsharing record 9313 already exists and is awaiting curator review. The public page hides its detailed fields. The correct action is to complete and maintain that record. re3data registration is the next target. Its current minimum requirements concern a research-data focus, a legal entity with a sustainable organisational framework, publicly explained access and terms of use. A functioning research-data service must be demonstrated accurately; publication records alone do not establish research-data holdings.

The existing repository user agreement and privacy notice need review. The agreement includes generic US-law and hosting language and restrictions on reuse and systematic retrieval that may conflict with the proposed metadata and item licensing approach. The adopted UIST policies should expressly replace inconsistent repository terms from their effective date. Existing licences and deposit permissions require their own rights assessment; adoption must not silently rewrite them.

## Adoption route

UIST's published statute assigns the Senate authority to adopt general acts, rules and instructions and gives the Rector responsibilities for institutional legality and working arrangements. Please confirm whether the final repository policies require Senate adoption, a delegated decision or another procedure under the currently applicable statute. Rector approval of development does not, by itself, enact these policies.

The proposed sequence is rector review, specialist review by the competent legal and personal data officers, confirmation of operational commitments by the service and technical owners, adoption by the competent authority, and publication of the approved versions. Editorial corrections may be coordinated by the repository owner; changes to institutional commitments require the authorised decision route.

The University also publishes rules on electronic publishing and a digital library. The legal service should determine whether the repository policies complement or fall within those rules, and confirm the published statute's current applicability. Research-data deposits should not be placed under textbook publication procedures without an explicit institutional decision.

No fee arrangement, retention duration, curation response time, hosting location or certification is assumed by the drafts. These matters must be confirmed where they affect public statements. The University's public personal data page currently lists a protection officer; the appointment and repository remit should be reconfirmed before publication.

## Work that can proceed under development approval

The development team can prepare publication routes and links, implement configuration for approved policy information, improve machine-readable metadata, prepare read-only readiness checks, and verify interfaces and access controls. It can prepare the registry record and supporting evidence. These actions do not choose licences or create preservation commitments.

The server administrator can maintain the live Handle service, document migration coverage and continuity checks, coordinate the approved deployment and supply evidence of hosting, backup, restore, fixity and access-control arrangements. After policy adoption, the development team can publish the approved text and corresponding machine-readable links. Submission should use the final verified statements and existing institutional arrangements.
