# SWISSUbase source register

> Dated comparative research for the earlier drafting stage. The [current four-page pack](../dabar_simple_2026-10-06/README.md) was accepted by the rector, as reported on 6 October 2026. Earlier UIST approval recommendations below are historical; comparator rules remain source observations and do not impose new UIST requirements.

**Review date:** 3 October 2026 (Europe/Skopje)

**Scope.** This register records primary SWISSUbase, consortium, DSU and FORS sources reviewed for the University UIST EDEN repository policy work. It records what the source says and whether it was available during review. Swiss law, Swiss institutional ownership rules and Swissubase contracts are evidence for drafting and comparison only; they are not assumed to govern EDEN in North Macedonia.

## Coverage inventory and unavailable material

The reviewed public inventory covers the platform, Terms of Use, platform privacy, deposit/download contracts and annex, preservation, backup, versioning, security, infrastructure, API/content negotiation, curation, DSUs, governance, selected DSU/FORS policies, release notes and live OAI-PMH. The resource page also exposes user guides for projects, datasets, metadata, licences, access/download/reuse, workflow, roles and versioning; these were used as operational guides where relevant.

The following material was not available as a fully machine-readable primary source in this review and should be treated as an evidence gap rather than inferred:

* The Preservation Policy appendices were inaccessible as page text in the isolated browser tab, but were subsequently downloaded, extracted and visually rendered locally. They are a five-page July 2025 appendix: OAIS responsibilities/functional entities and definitions; CC BY, BY-SA, BY-NC, BY-NC-SA, BY-ND, BY-NC-ND and CC0; DSU-specific restricted licences through download contracts; and Swiss federal/cantonal legal-context references. They do not establish an EDEN legal basis or an unconditional preservation guarantee.
* The “complete endpoint documentation” for authenticated REST operations is available on request, according to the API page, but was not publicly exposed in the reviewed pages.
* The UniNE policy has version 2.0 but blank last-modified/publisher/introduced fields.
* The FORS Data Protection Policy PDF has no visible version/date in the inspected body.
* SWISSUbase’s public source pages do not expose a single complete data-processing-subprocessor register; the Deposit Contract refers to a list and notice/objection process.
* The public OAI Identify response advertises `deletedRecord=no`; no separate live OAI evidence was found that tombstone records are harvested as deleted records.
* No Handle registration or live Handle resolution was evidenced. The reviewed sources use DOI/DataCite for datasets. EDEN’s repository-specific Handle work must therefore remain separate.
