# Balkan university policy research for UIST EDEN

> Dated comparative research for the earlier drafting stage. The [current four-page pack](../dabar_simple_2026-10-06/README.md) was accepted by the rector, as reported on 6 October 2026. Earlier UIST approval recommendations below are historical; comparator rules remain source observations and do not impose new UIST requirements.

Research date: **3 October 2026**. Prepared as evidence for rector-review drafts, not as adopted university policy or a legal opinion. Only official institutional, government/regulator, and intergovernmental sources support the findings below. No institution was contacted and no submission was made.

## Findings that affect the draft package

1. **UIST has its own relevant rules already.** Its published statute, publishing rules, and data-protection pages should be the first institutional anchors. EDEN policy adoption needs a confirmed route through the competent university bodies; development approval and the already-approved Handle.net process are separate from adoption of these policy drafts.
2. **UKIM has a current 2026–2030 Open Science Policy.** The March 2022 policy is a historical predecessor, not the best current comparator. The new policy is a strong North Macedonian example of distinguishing publications, data, software, licences, PIDs, and legal exceptions.
3. **An operating publication repository is not proof of a research-data policy.** UKLO, UGD, and the University of Tetovo expose policy pages saying that no policies have been defined. These are gaps, not policies to copy. SEEU's official library confirms a thesis repository; a separate public research-data policy was not located in this review.
4. **DABAR is the most concise regional operational-policy model.** It distinguishes public metadata reuse from reuse of files, administrator checks from scientific peer review, embargoes from deposit, and withdrawal from destruction.
5. **Ljubljana and Maribor provide data-specific institutional models.** Their DMP, justified access restriction, documentation, and stewardship practices are more relevant to re3data than publication-only repositories. Their national laws and funding obligations must not be imported as North Macedonian requirements.
6. **ADP demonstrates why EDEN should describe its actual curation level.** Basic deposit checks and bit preservation are different from discipline-specific scientific quality assessment. A small repository can state honest, limited checks without claiming the intensive curation of a national social-science archive.

## UIST: institution and internal authority

### UIST statute

Official index: [Legal Acts](https://uist.edu.mk/legal-acts/).

Document: **Статут на Универзитетот за информатички науки и технологии „Св. Апостол Павле“ Охрид**. The university index links a document numbered **01-156/1, 8 February 2019**; its 2026 upload path does not establish a new adoption date. Language: Macedonian. [Official PDF](https://uist.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Статут-на-УИНТ-Свети-Апостол-Павле-01-156-1-08.02.2019-година.pdf).

Relevant provisions, paraphrased:

- Articles 1 and 4 describe the university as a public institution with legal personality and its constituent units as lacking separate legal personality.
- Article 3 provides the Macedonian name and the English name **University of information science and technology “St. Paul the Apostle” Ohrid**. The current website uses **University of Information Science and Technology “St. Paul the Apostle” Ohrid**. Confirm the exact legal display name and approved English spelling before enactment.
- Articles 113–115 describe Senate authority and its adoption of general acts, decisions, rules, instructions, and conclusions within its competence.
- Article 148 allows the Rector to establish commissions/working groups, which report with proposals; Article 152 concerns the Rector's responsibility for legality and conformity with the statute.

**Application:** ask the Rector/legal service to confirm the approving body and legal basis for the EDEN rules. A draft may propose a small repository oversight working group; it must not state that an EDEN board, committee, or data steward already exists.

### Existing publishing and digital-library rules

Document: **Правилник за организирање на издавачката дејност на УИНТ**, consolidated text incorporating **23 December 2020 and 28 September 2022**, Macedonian, 24 pages. [Official PDF](https://uist.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Правилник-за-организирање-на-издавачката-дејност-на-УИНТ-пречистен-текст-23.12.2020-и-28.09.2022..pdf).

The electronic-publishing section on printed page 11 says that electronic university editions follow the procedure for printed editions; it contemplates a UIST digital library and a proposed digital-library rulebook prepared by the Rector's Office, covering storage, access, use, electronic editions, and copyright. Earlier sections assign responsibilities to a publishing commission. These provisions concern university publishing and the digital library; they do not establish a standalone research-data curation service.

**Application:** have legal staff determine whether EDEN is governed by these digital-library provisions, a new complementary repository rulebook, or both. Avoid accidentally subjecting every research-data deposit to the textbook publishing process. Do not add fees or membership requirements merely because the publishing rules mention them.

### Existing ethics and data-protection anchors

- The [Legal Acts index](https://uist.edu.mk/legal-acts/) links **Етички кодекс на УИНТ**, dated **25 June 2021**, Macedonian. [Official PDF](https://uist.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Етички-кодекс-на-УИНТ-25.06.2021.pdf). This review verified its publication link, not all its substantive clauses; refer depositors to applicable institutional ethics rules rather than inventing ethics approval procedures.
- [Personal Data Protection](https://uist.edu.mk/info-center/protection-personal-data/), English, no displayed version/adoption date: identifies the university as controller at Partizanska bb, 6000 Ohrid, with **contact@uist.edu.mk**. It lists rights and university request handling, purpose-based retention, authorised employee access, and external-sharing conditions. It currently names **Jordanka Cvetkoska–Atanasova**, **jordanka.c.atanasova@uist.edu.mk**, as Data Protection Officer. Confirm this appointment/contact with the Rector before an EDEN notice is adopted.
- [UIST Privacy Policy](https://uist.edu.mk/info-center/uist-privacy-policy/), English, undated: expressly limits its stated scope to the main website and describes server logs and a one-day ordinary log deletion rule. Its simultaneous assertion that no personal data are collected needs care given its own IP/log description. This is not evidence that EDEN uses identical logging or deletion practices.
- [UIST Repository Privacy Statement](https://repository.uist.edu.mk/info/privacy), English, last updated **4 May 2023**: uses generic “Company”, under-13, and business-transfer language. Treat it as an existing notice to reconcile, not a legally validated EDEN template. EDEN needs a notice describing its actual accounts, public author metadata, uploads, support requests, logs, recipients, hosting, retention, and rights routes.

## North Macedonian university comparators

### UKIM — current institutional Open Science Policy

Document: **Политика за отворена наука на Универзитетот „Св. Кирил и Методиј“ во Скопје (2026–2030)**, cover **January 2026**, published in **Универзитетски гласник No. 752, 2 March 2026**, Macedonian, 15 PDF pages. [Official university faculty-hosted PDF](https://pf.ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/glasnik-752.pdf), linked from [Acts of the University](https://pf.ukim.edu.mk/akti-na-univerzitetot/).

The policy covers publications, raw/processed data, metadata/documentation, software, and OER. Section 5 calls for accepted publication versions to be deposited subject to publisher/legal requirements, with deposit ideally at publication and no later than six months. It recommends CC BY/equivalent for publications/OER, OSI-approved software licences, and CC0/CC BY for data where lawful. It recommends PIDs, reusable preservation formats, and DMPs. Legal, privacy/confidentiality, and third-party contractual restrictions can justify embargo, limited access, or anonymisation. It assigns coordination/support to an Open Science Centre and university services, uses annual monitoring, and currently favours support over formal sanctions.

**Application:** useful structure and licence distinctions; its six-month deadline and institutional mandate are UKIM choices, not legal minimums for EDEN. Make EDEN's mandatory/encouraged deposit status explicit and subject to rector approval. Cite this current source instead of describing only 2022 practices.

Historical predecessor: **Open Science Policy at Ss. Cyril and Methodius University in Skopje, March 2022**. [Official archived Macedonian PDF](https://archive.ukim.edu.mk/dokumenti_m/Open_Science_Policy_UKIM_March_2022-mk.pdf). The old www.ukim.edu.mk English PDF URL currently redirects to HTML; do not distribute it as a working PDF link. Historical policy coverage included FAIR, registry inclusion, and legal restrictions.

### UGD — publication repository; missing published policy

[Official Research page](https://www.ugd.edu.mk/en/research/), English, undated, describes UGD Academic Repository as collecting university research papers/results and electronic theses; it distinguishes the university's journal platform. [Repository Policies](https://eprints.ugd.edu.mk/policies.html), English, no version, explicitly displays **“No policies have been defined for this repository.”** [User Documentation](https://eprints.ugd.edu.mk/help/), English, undated, describes deposit verification and an editorial buffer.

**Application:** evidence of a submission/moderation workflow, not evidence of an adopted data policy, preservation guarantee, or approved default licence. Do not reproduce old EPrints help examples exposing personal addresses or describing automatic website-copying as EDEN practice.

### UKLO — publication repository; missing published policy

[UKLO Repository](https://eprints.uklo.edu.mk/), English, undated, provides browse/search, institutional divisions, and OAI-PMH. [Repository Policies](https://eprints.uklo.edu.mk/policies.html) was read through agent-browser on 3 October 2026 and explicitly states that no policies have been defined. [Contact page](https://eprints.uklo.edu.mk/contact.html) identifies a repository administrator.

**Application:** useful visibility and support-contact example, but not a substantive policy precedent. A link labelled Repository Policies is insufficient unless it contains real approved rules.

### University of Tetovo — same caution

[Repository Policies](https://eprints.unite.edu.mk/policies.html), English, undated, likewise contains the no-policies-defined EPrints placeholder. It does not establish repository conditions or a research-data policy.

### SEEU — thesis/library evidence, no data policy verified

[Library “Max van der Stoel”](https://www.seeu.edu.mk/en/current-students/library), English, undated, identifies a university library and links a thesis repository. [Important Documents](https://seeu.edu.mk/mk/about/important-documents), Macedonian, lists a statute consolidated **June 2025** and prior versions. The [SEEU Review page](https://www.seeu.edu.mk/en/research/seeu-review) describes a journal; journal submission/copyright rules must not be recast as repository-deposit conditions. This search did not locate an approved, separately published institutional research-data/repository operational policy.

**Application:** use the library support model and clearly marked evidence gap; do not conclude that SEEU has no internal rules.

## Other Balkan comparators

### DABAR / University of Zagreb University Computing Centre (SRCE)

[Repository policies](https://dabar.srce.hr/en/repository-policies), English, no displayed adoption date/version; live page read using agent-browser. DABAR is a Croatian academic repository infrastructure supporting several object types, including datasets: [Supported digital objects](https://dabar.srce.hr/en/objects) includes a research-data metadata specification **v1.1, 30 January 2019**, and [Advanced search](https://dabar.srce.hr/en/advanced-search) separates datasets, DMPs, software, and access categories.

Policy substance: metadata is CC0 except specified personal identifiers/email; files retain their deposited licences. Accredited institutional members/delegates deposit, administrators verify eligibility/scope/layout/format/spam, and depositors remain responsible for authenticity. Embargoed files are released later. The preservation policy promises indefinite retention, original bitstreams, backups, attempted migration, documented changes, and transfer to a suitable archive if the repository closes. Withdrawal reasons include legal/copyright violations and security; persistent URLs resolve to tombstone citations.

**Application:** adopt the separation of metadata/files and corrections/withdrawal. Do not copy Croatian identifiers, a national-repository label, an indefinite-storage guarantee, or blanket depositor-only liability. Metadata and tombstones must be suppressible where necessary to protect rights or comply with law. [Privacy notice](https://dabar.srce.hr/en/privacy-notice) is also useful for distinguishing institutional controller from technical processor; EDEN's real arrangements must be established first.

### University of Ljubljana — research-data obligations and practical access models

[Research data management](https://www.uni-lj.si/en/study/doctoral-study/research-data-management), English, current institutional guidance with no displayed version; linked to doctoral rules. Doctoral students prepare and update a DMP with their supervisor, and publish data in a suitable repository under FAIR principles. Restrictions require a DMP explanation; metadata should describe unavailable/restricted data. Exceptions include personal/sensitive data, intellectual property, and endangered areas/groups/species. A discipline-specific repository is preferred where suitable, with the UL repository another option. The guidance calls for reproducibility documentation, rights checks, a PID citation, and deposit by final dissertation submission.

[RUL manual — Access types](https://repozitorij.uni-lj.si/pomoc.php?id=zaposleni.vrstadostopa&lang=eng), English, undated, distinguishes immediate publication; public metadata with embargoed files; public metadata with permanently restricted files; and restricted metadata plus files. Organisations/libraries and administrators manage access, with optional requests.

**Application:** make access choices explicit per file and record. A future EDEN DMP template can capture the reason, review date, and decision owner for restrictions. Do not import Slovenian ownership law or doctoral mandates into UIST; these need UIST-specific decisions.

### University of Maribor — current Open Science Policy and stewardship

[University of Maribor Open Science Policy](https://www.um.si/en/research/open-science/university-of-maribor-open-science-policy/), English, adopted by Senate **28 October 2025**, Decision **030-10/2025/10/515-MP**. It links open science to research, education, infrastructure, and public engagement. For research producing data, a DMP is required and updated; FAIR, metadata, PIDs, publication links, and justified restrictions are explicit. It allows anonymised or controlled access where appropriate and assigns policy design, coordination, advisory, data-steward, and legal/technical roles. The institution maintains a repository and works with other data centres; oversight reviews the policy every two years as needed.

**Application:** combine roles proportionately at a small university while assigning accountability. One appointed repository manager with IT, DPO/legal, and academic escalation support can be a realistic initial arrangement. Do not imply that EDEN currently has Maribor's staff network or that Slovenian open-science legislation governs it.

### University of Ljubljana Social Science Data Archives (ADP)

[Acquisition criteria](https://www.adp.fdv.uni-lj.si/en/criteria-for-acquisition/), English, undated. ADP evaluates collection fit, scientific value, legality/ethics, reuse potential, and curation costs. It differentiates active long-term curation with scientific quality appraisal from self-deposit with basic checks and bit preservation; documentation and usable formats are important. It verifies sharing rights and protection of personal information.

[Access types](https://www.adp.fdv.uni-lj.si/en/access-types/), English, undated, separates open downloads, registered public-use files, research files requiring an application and confidentiality review, and sensitive files used in a supervised secure room. [General Terms of Use](https://www.adp.fdv.uni-lj.si/en/terms-of-use/), English, undated, includes individual account use, confidentiality, citation, issue reporting, and file-specific restrictions; it also contains a one-year download-use/deletion condition.

**Application:** describe EDEN's actual curation tier; offer sensitive-data deposit only when both governance and technical protection exist. Do not copy ADP's secure-room promises, Slovenian researcher-status criteria, or one-year deletion rule into open CC-licensed EDEN reuse terms. An open licence and restricted-access agreement need a consistent scope.

ADP publishes a **Digital Preservation Policy**, with an official bibliographic record dated **28 May 2021**: [2021 policy record](https://www.adp.fdv.uni-lj.si/publikacije/digital-preservation-policy-2/). The browser and some direct page requests encountered Anubis access denial, while indexed official acquisition/access/terms content remained readable. The full current preservation document was not verified; no detailed claims here rely on the 2017 third-party mirror. [CoreTrustSeal assessment, 7 March 2022](https://www.coretrustseal.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/20220307-slovenian-social-science-data-archives-adp_final.pdf) is a historical assessment, not proof that EDEN meets any certification requirement.

### University of Belgrade Computer Centre (RCUB) — service governance

[Policy for Open Access to Research Infrastructures at the University of Belgrade Computer Centre: TRAP-RCUB IT Solution and Organizational Model for Institutional or Thematic Repositories](https://www.rcub.bg.ac.rs/documents/TRAP-Open_Access_Policy.pdf), English, six pages, no adoption date visible in the PDF. A [later official wiki presentation](https://repowiki.rcub.bg.ac.rs/index.php/Policy_for_Transparent_Access_to_Research_Infrastructures_at_the_Computer_Centre_of_the_University_of_Belgrade%3A_TRAP-RCUB_IT_solution_and_organizational_model_for_the_implementation_of_institutional_or_thematic_repositories) is titled Policy for Transparent Access; do not assume the two editions are identical.

The PDF assigns supervision to a director, implementation/management to the TRAP team, institutional responsibilities to university users, and financial/legal support roles. It covers service agreements, operating/maintenance periods, confidentiality, complaints, and IP. The service policy leaves content IP details to the individual repositories.

**Application:** separate the university's responsibility for repository operations from depositors' responsibility for content. EDEN should publish a complaint/escalation route and keep technical responsibilities in an internal operations schedule. Do not copy a 99% uptime guarantee, commercial fees, agreement periods, Serbian law, or RCUB's complaint response periods without EDEN capability and approval.

## Authoritative North Macedonian legal checks

### Personal data

[AZLP — Laws](https://azlp.mk/en/pdpa/regulations-and-documents/laws/) lists **Law on Personal Data Protection, Official Gazette 42/2020**, and amendment **294/2021**. Its [English 2020 PDF](https://azlp.mk/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/lpdp_2020.pdf) is explicitly an **unofficial translation**, not a consolidated translation; Macedonian official text and amendments control.

Relevant provisions: Article 3 applies to controllers/processors established in North Macedonia; Articles 9–10 require accountable, purpose-limited, proportionate, secure, lawful processing. Article 13 concerns special-category information. Articles 17–18 require transparency about controller/contact, purposes/basis, recipients, retention, rights, and relevant transfers. Articles 36–38 cover security and breach duties; 48 onward concerns transfers. Article 86 requires safeguards for research/archiving, including minimisation and appropriate de-identification. Research is not a blanket permission to disclose identifiable records.

[AZLP controller FAQ](https://azlp.mk/en/controllers/frequently-asked-questions-and-answers/) clarifies that ordinary retention depends on purpose and longer scientific/archival storage requires safeguards; it does not establish a universal repository retention minimum. **Draft decision:** UIST DPO/legal review must settle EDEN processing purposes/bases, retention criteria, hosting/processor arrangements, transfers, and rights handling. Cite North Macedonian law; evaluate EU GDPR applicability separately if facts trigger it.

### Copyright and related/database rights

[Ministry of Culture and Tourism — Laws](https://kultura.gov.mk/mk-MK/informacii-od-javen-karakter/zakoni) publishes the law and amendments plus an **unofficial consolidated Macedonian text**. [Consolidated DOC](https://portal.mdt.gov.mk/post-body-files/zakoni-kultura-file-ulbG.doc), read locally, lists **115/2010, 140/2010, 51/2011, 147/2013, 154/2015, 27/2016**. It is not a certified statement that no subsequent changes exist.

The text distinguishes copyright from possession, recognises original scientific/software/other works, excludes simple facts/data from copyright, and also regulates database rights. Articles 63, 65 and 70 distinguish written exclusive/non-exclusive permissions and their scope; Article 67 protects against transfer of copyright as a whole or moral rights. [WIPO Lex base law](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/10641) warns that its base text has been amended; [2016 amendment](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/16914) provides provenance.

**Draft decision:** obtain a clear non-exclusive repository permission, separately from the public reuse licence. Require authority for coauthor/third-party/publisher rights; do not declare all data public domain, all student research university-owned, or moral rights waived. Have university legal staff confirm electronic acceptance/signature sufficiency and the scope of preservation/format-migration permission.

### Higher-education authority/current legislation

The Rector should ask the university legal service to verify the current law and amendments and the published UIST statute's continued applicability before adoption. Official [MON notice, 9 February 2026](https://mon.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/soopstenija/noviot-zakon-za-visoko-obrazovanie-dostapen-za-komentari-i-sugestii) described draft higher-education/science laws under consultation; [MON update, 21 May 2026](https://mon.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/soopstenija/sektorska-rabotna-grupa-za-obrazovanie-vrabotuvanje-i-socijalna-politika-intenzivirani-reformi-za-strateskite-prioriteti) still described bills heading to Parliament. These notices do not prove enactment by 3 October. Accordingly this review does not assert that the 2018 framework or the 2026 proposal is the definitive current consolidated law.

## Rector decision list and recommended starting choices

1. **Authority and institutional owner:** confirm the university legal name, approving body, and relationship to the existing publishing/digital-library rules. Recommended: university-owned service with a designated accountable office and an appointed manager; legal/DPO/IT/academic escalation as required.
2. **Scope:** explicitly accept UIST research datasets, their methods/README/codebooks, research software, and related publications. Decide student and collaborative/external deposits. Recommended: UIST-affiliated outputs and approved collaborations; no general unrestricted public upload mandate.
3. **Curation:** approve an honest initial service: eligibility, metadata completeness, rights/access flags, documentation, format, and technical safety checks. Scientific merit/accuracy remains with researchers unless a separate review is commissioned.
4. **Sensitive data:** decide whether EDEN may hold restricted identifiable/sensitive research information. Recommended initial scope: open/non-sensitive or appropriately de-identified deposits, with referrals for unsuitable datasets; enable sensitive controlled deposits only after documented safeguards and decision authority exist.
5. **Licences:** approve separate permissions for repository operations, metadata reuse, and object reuse. Recommended: CC0 public descriptive metadata where lawful/authorised; encourage CC BY 4.0 or CC0 for data and CC BY for original publications; appropriate OSI licence for software; accommodate publisher/third-party restrictions. Record the rights holder and licence, and do not relicense legacy files automatically.
6. **Access:** approve open, embargo, restricted, and metadata-only definitions that match implemented controls. Recommended: open by default where lawful, with documented restriction reasons and periodic review; redact metadata when disclosure itself is unsafe.
7. **Preservation/retention:** decide the funded promise and who verifies backup/restoration/fixity. Recommended: preserve accepted outputs while EDEN operates, with an approved review/continuity plan and stated limits. No universal ten-year or perpetual promise is established by the examined North Macedonian sources.
8. **Takedown/corrections:** designate decision and appeal contacts. Recommended: prompt temporary restriction on a credible rights/privacy/security concern, recorded review, proportionate outcome, and a minimal persistent tombstone where lawful. New versions/corrections should preserve history; no silent replacement of materially changed datasets.
9. **Privacy and security:** confirm the DPO, controller, processor/hosting arrangements, notice, retention criteria, and incident escalation. Technical logs and audit records need a justified schedule; the main website's one-day log rule is not evidence for EDEN's configuration.
10. **Continuity:** designate who initiates cessation review, notification, export, lawful transfer, and PID maintenance. Recommended: an approved process and documented authority to transfer, not a fictional named successor archive.
11. **Publication and review:** approve Macedonian and English policy versions, authoritative-language rule, version/effective-date display, stable policy URLs, and a periodic review. Recommended: annual operational review and policy review when law/service scope changes; adopt only after the institutional approval route is settled.

## Technical work these findings support before policy adoption

Within the already-authorised development scope, the team can build configurable metadata/record-type/licence/access fields; embargo and restricted-file enforcement; public/private metadata boundaries; safe export and harvesting; version/history and tombstones; policy-version acceptance records; rights/ethics check fields; support/contact slots; monitoring/fixity hooks; and evidence reports. These are recommendations, not a code audit.

Keep policy-dependent choices configurable or disabled until adopted: public default licences, compulsory deposit rules, retention promises, restricted sensitive-data acceptance, automatic de-embargo, named institutional contacts, and migration/successor permissions. A page bearing draft policies must be clearly marked draft; a registry submission must state current approved practice, not future aspirations.

## Evidence limitations

The report does not infer unpublished rules from absence in search results. UIST documents were read from the official site; their upload dates can differ from adoption dates. Some archived UKIM URLs redirect, and some ADP pages were blocked by Anubis; those limitations are recorded above. No current consolidated North Macedonian higher-education law, legal retention minimum, EDEN funding commitment, sensitive-data capacity, legal signature method, or archive succession agreement was established by this research. Each needs institutional confirmation or a separate technical/legal check.
