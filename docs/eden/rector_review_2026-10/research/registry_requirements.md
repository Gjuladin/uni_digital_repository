# Registry requirements and submission readiness

> Dated comparative research for the earlier drafting stage. The [current four-page pack](../dabar_simple_2026-10-06/README.md) was accepted by the rector, as reported on 6 October 2026. Earlier UIST approval recommendations below are historical; comparator rules remain source observations and do not impose new UIST requirements.

Research checked on 3 October 2026. Dedicated registry research; no registration was submitted, no external record was edited, and no organisation was contacted.

## Findings for the rector pack

re3data's current minimum admission rules concern the repository's research-data purpose, sustainable legal operator, transparent access, and terms of use. They do **not** require operational Handles or DOIs, OAI-PMH, CoreTrustSeal, an open licence, a particular software platform, or completion of every desirable preservation feature. This is distinct from the richer metadata used to describe an accepted repository. The current official documentation is **Metadata Schema version 4.0, August 2023**, linked by the live schema page; the live API also identifies 4.0 as current. [Official schema page](https://www.re3data.org/schema), [version 4.0 documentation, section 1.3, p. 6](https://doi.org/10.48440/re3.014), [official API documentation](https://www.re3data.org/api/doc/).

The main unresolved re3data evidence issue is **actual research-data scope**. UIST's public homepage currently advertises publications, theses, conference papers and project outputs, with publication-based collections. That does not by itself evidence a research-data service. Technical discovery working in EDEN is useful but does not settle that scope question. A real, appropriately licensed dataset deposit, with a usable data file and descriptive metadata, is strong practical evidence; re3data does not state a numerical minimum of one dataset. A functioning portal that harvests research-data metadata can also be eligible. Do not relabel ordinary articles or theses as datasets to obtain registration. [UIST homepage](https://repository.uist.edu.mk/), [re3data suggestion policy](https://www.re3data.org/suggest), [current registration policy](https://doi.org/10.48440/re3.014).

The existing [UIST FAIRsharing record 9313](https://fairsharing.org/9313) was inspected in a logged-out agent-browser session. It reports that it is awaiting curator review and identifies the hidden resource as UIST Digital Repository. **Update this record; do not create a duplicate.** Detailed field values and the exact outstanding checks could not be inspected without its authorised logged-in session. Older local statements that no record exists are stale. The user-provided warning is evidence of missing recommendations, not proof that mandatory fields are incomplete.

## 1. re3data: the four minimum requirements

The official v4.0 registration policy, section 1.3 on printed page 6, provides the authoritative current list. The live suggestion page expresses the same substance using three bullets, combining access and terms in one bullet. Older schema v2.1 lists an English GUI requirement; **English GUI is not one of the four requirements in v4.0**. English-facing documentation remains sensible for international editorial review.

| Current criterion | What the public repository must demonstrate | UIST position and evidence still needed | Owner / submission classification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Focus on research data | A repository offering research data and metadata, or a service harvesting research-data metadata. Experiments, measurements, surveys and interviews are examples. | Public homepage describes publications/theses. Confirm research-data service remit and permitted deposits; demonstrate genuine dataset holdings or a functioning data portal. An EDEN assessment of SWISSUbase is evidence about SWISSUbase, not a UIST dataset. | **Potential blocker**. Rector approves service remit; development implements supported dataset workflows; a depositor supplies rights-cleared data. |
| Operated by a legal entity with an organisational framework that provides sustainability | Identify the legal university operator and responsible service structure. A university is an expressly given example. | UIST identity and university links are visible. State named service ownership, staff responsibilities, contact and continuity arrangements without claiming unfunded guarantees. | **Minimum criterion**. Rector establishes governance; development exposes already established institutional facts. |
| Clarify access conditions to repository and research data | Explain access to catalogue/metadata, access to data files, and any restrictions, embargoes, registration or requests. Upload conditions should also be clear. | Public browsing is observable; site behaviour does not substitute for an intelligible access statement. Document repository access, file access and deposit eligibility separately. | **Blocker until clear**. Rector approves rules; development implements and displays accurate rules. |
| Provide terms of use | Public, coherent conditions governing service/content use, including rights and licensing arrangements. | Terms exist at `/info/end-user-agreement`, but restrictive boilerplate conflicts with intended harvesting/reuse and continuity. Review rather than claim policies are absent. | **Blocker if incoherent**. Rector/legal reviewer approves replacement; development publishes approved text. |

Sources for all four rows: [v4.0 documentation](https://doi.org/10.48440/re3.014) (direct official PDF successfully indexed during research: [GFZ full text](https://gfzpublic.gfz.de/pubman/item/item_5022309_2/component/file_5022322/re3data-schema-documentation-V4-0.pdf)); [live suggestion page](https://www.re3data.org/suggest). The PDF endpoint intermittently returned 403 on subsequent fetches; the official DOI and live policy are the durable references.

## 2. Admission rules, application fields and schema requirements are different layers

### Live suggestion form, inspected without submitting

The form can be read using a JavaScript-capable browser. Its **Required information** pane contains repository name; name language; repository URL; description; description language; data licences; and suggester's email. Name, URL, description and email have required HTML input attributes. The two language selectors default to English and offer other languages, including Macedonian. A CAPTCHA must be answered by the submitting operator. No account login was needed to read the suggestion form; do not portray a UIST-owned re3data account as an official admission prerequisite.

The data-licence collection is inside that required pane. Its form prototype has licence name and URL labels marked required; options include Creative Commons, CC0, Copyrights, other and none. Therefore **a specific open licence is not a prerequisite**, but a truthful data rights/usage entry should be prepared. Do not treat the UI's initially empty dynamic collection as evidence that licence information can be omitted: server-side submission validation was not tested. The current XML schema requires at least one `dataLicense` entry with name and URL. [Live form](https://www.re3data.org/suggest), [v4.0 XML schema](https://schema.re3data.org/4-0/re3dataV4-0.xsd).

The form labels its remaining panes General, Institutions, Terms and Standards as optional. Optional form entry does **not** waive the institutional/access/use criteria: editors extract evidence from the website. Provide policy URLs and representative dataset URLs in the comments even if the form does not force them.

### Schema cardinality is for the registry record

The v4.0 XSD requires repository name/URL; registry identifiers; repository type; language; subject; provider type; institution; repository access; data access; data licence; data upload; entry date; and last update. Editors construct and enrich that record; applicants should not invent re3data-issued identifiers or editorial dates. Description is optional in the XSD but required in the suggestion form, illustrating why these layers cannot be conflated.

`policy`, metadata/database licence, software, API, PID system, metadata standard, certificate and quality-management entries permit zero occurrences. This does not erase the substantive terms-of-use criterion. APIs, if described, include an API documentation field. The schema's `databaseLicense` refers to **repository metadata**, while `dataLicense` refers to **research data**. A DSpace software licence is neither of these. [Official v4.0 XSD](https://schema.re3data.org/4-0/re3dataV4-0.xsd), [documentation](https://doi.org/10.48440/re3.014).

### Editorial process and honest completion

Anyone may suggest a repository. The international Editorial Board analyses its website, enhances the supplied metadata using an indexing handbook, and a second editor reviews the information before publication. A submitted form is not acceptance or public indexing. No guaranteed acceptance time was evidenced in the reviewed official material. After publication, capture the stable record identifier and verify any EDEN lookup against the public record. [Registration workflow in FAQ](https://www.re3data.org/faq), [schema documentation, section 1.4](https://doi.org/10.48440/re3.014).

## 4. Existing UIST policy evidence and conflicts

Public URLs already exist: [End User Agreement](https://repository.uist.edu.mk/info/end-user-agreement), [Privacy Statement](https://repository.uist.edu.mk/info/privacy). Their existence must be recognised; their accuracy and institutional adoption were not established by this research.

The End User Agreement is dated 4 May 2023. It asserts United States hosting and US intellectual-property framing; describes personal/non-commercial use; prohibits systematic retrieval to compile collections, databases or directories without permission; and disclaims continuing support and responsibility for data loss. These are material tensions with metadata harvesting, item-specific open licences and a sustainable repository mission. The actual hosting location needs administrator evidence. An approved new policy should expressly replace conflicting prior repository terms and explain precedence of item licences, rather than leave both texts online as competing rules. This is a policy approval task, not an autonomous technical wording change. [Existing agreement](https://repository.uist.edu.mk/info/end-user-agreement).

## 5. FAIRsharing: interpret the warning correctly

FAIRsharing distinguishes mandatory, recommended and optional fields. Records with missing mandatory fields are not forwarded to curators; missing recommendations trigger advice. New records remain hidden until checked and approved. This is a record curation workflow, **not repository certification**. Its resource-status field and third-party certification/community-badge information are separate concepts. Record 9313 is already in that workflow. [Minimal curation requirements](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/how-to-update-a-record#minimal-curation-requirements), [record indicators](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/about-our-records/record-icons), [certification/badges](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/additional-information/certification-and-community-badges).

| User's warning field | Official expectation | UIST action / approval boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organisation links (listed twice) | At least one maintaining and one funding organisation are recommended. An organisation may have multiple roles. | Add UIST with `maintains`; add UIST with `funds` only if its operational/in-kind support is factual. Do not invent a grant or external funder. Repetition in the warning is not proof that two distinct organisations are needed. [Organisations](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/organisations-and-grants), [roles](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/organisations-and-grants/organisation-role). |
| Licences | Recommended for all records; describe resource content, not the manuscript describing the resource or the underlying application software. Licence lists may be indicative or exhaustive. | Record actual accepted/recommended **content** licences and approved UIST terms. Enable the complete-list toggle only if exhaustive. Do not replace unknown deposit rights with blanket CC BY. Rector decides licence policy; developer records real item licence URLs. [Licence guidance](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/licences-and-support-links/licences). |
| Publications | Recommended; publications must describe the resource itself, and preprints qualify. | Add an existing article/preprint/report about UIST Digital Repository only if it exists and is a valid resource reference. Hosted scholarly papers do not qualify merely because the repository contains them. No such resource publication was evidenced here. Leave absent honestly pending a real publication. [Publications](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/publications). |
| Citations | Mark one or more genuine resource publications with the citation toggle. | This warning does not mean fabricating citations or counting item citation strings. If no suitable resource publication exists, leave this recommendation unfulfilled. A website citation guide can still be useful to users. [Citing your resource](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/publications#citing-your-resource). |
| Data curation | Describe actual manual, automated, both, none or not-found curation. Database-condition dropdowns are mandatory; supporting notes/URLs are optional. | Do not infer manual review from DSpace installation. Confirm deposit validation and review workflow; link the approved procedure. `None` means explicitly no curation, whereas `not found` means insufficient information. The warning may reflect an uninformative value; hidden record fields need the maintainer's review. [Database conditions](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/additional-information/database-conditions#data-curation). |
| Record associations | Formats/terminologies are recommended, but identifier-type declaration has a mandatory component. | Use `implements` for identifiers actually assigned by this repository. UIST has verified live Handle sample resolution under `20.500.15029`; declare that capability with the dated evidence and keep new-item minting claims scoped to their own checks. Add `outputs` relations to actually exported standards and accurate relationships to other resources. [Database relationships](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/associated-records/from-database-records), [identifier type](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/general-information/identifier-type). |

### Mandatory fields to recheck in the existing hidden record

Confirm name, homepage, standalone description, at least one contact and at least one precise object type, plus database identifier declaration and every database-condition dropdown. Object type should describe current content; FAIRsharing supports `publication` as a type, so a publication repository can be represented honestly there even while re3data's research-data focus still needs evidence. Contact is a person/team name and email; an ORCID alone is insufficient. [Name](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/general-information/record-name), [homepage](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/general-information/homepage), [description](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/general-information/description), [contact](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/general-information/contact-information), [object types](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/general-information/object-types).

The database-condition guidance covers access, curation, deposit, preservation, sustainability, links to related papers, private access for pre-publication review, data-owner contact and public versioning. Answer from production evidence. Do not mark versioning yes for invisible internal history, or data-owner contact yes merely for an author name/ORCID. Its mandatory-dropdown statement coexists with older text suggesting blank curation when unknown; follow the live form/current mandatory validation and truthful `not found` fallback, asking curators if the interface conflicts. [Database conditions](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/additional-information/database-conditions).

### Accurate association candidates

- **Handle identifier scheme:** UIST prefix `20.500.15029` is live and sample `/261` resolved publicly on 2026-10-06. Use `implements` for the assigned repository Handle scheme with [dated evidence](../../HANDLE_ACTIVATION.md); do not infer new-item minting or full migration coverage from this sample.
- **Dublin Core, DataCite metadata, schema.org:** `outputs` where deployed endpoints actually export those standards. Exporting DataCite XML is not DOI minting. Confirm the exact FAIRsharing standard record and metadata version before adding links.
- **ORCID/DOI references already attached to articles:** express output/cross-reference use as appropriate; this does not imply UIST mints those identifier schemes.
- **DSpace:** describe as software/associated tool, where available, rather than misclassifying software as a metadata standard.
- **re3data:** after the UIST record becomes public, add its correct identifier as a cross-reference where supported. Do not claim an association implying endorsement or data exchange without evidence.
- **Other repositories/collections:** use data-sharing or collection relationships only for actual arrangements. A design reference to SWISSUbase establishes no operational relationship.

The mandatory identifier declaration is about identifiers minted/created for the resource's content. FAIRsharing allows an internal-identifier declaration instead, so Handle completion is not inherently a FAIRsharing review blocker. A DOI FAIRsharing assigns to its own record after acceptance is not a dataset DOI service run by UIST. [Identifier-type guidance](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/general-information/identifier-type), [FAIRsharing homepage field and record DOI](https://fairsharing.gitbook.io/fairsharing/record-sections-and-fields/general-information/homepage).

## 6. Fastest credible route to re3data submission

| Track | Work now | Gate / owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical | Verify deployed metadata/API endpoints and document them; make genuine dataset types, metadata, licences, citation and restricted-file behaviour work; prepare public policy routes and links; collect production evidence. | Existing development approval applies. Policy routes must not publish unapproved drafts as adopted rules. |
| Rector policy | Approve research-data remit, operator/contact, deposit/access rules, content/licence selection, metadata harvesting/reuse permission, corrected terms, curation responsibility and achievable continuity statement. | Rector approval of document drafts is still needed. A limited coherent adopted statement can enable early submission while detailed stewardship plans mature. |
| Administrator | Maintain the live Handle service; document complete migration/redirect coverage and continuity checks; supply hosting, backups, restore and security facts. | Sample resolution verified on 2026-10-06. This track need not block submission if current identifier status is described honestly. |
| Content evidence | Identify a rights-cleared real research dataset with files, metadata, reuse terms and a live landing page, or demonstrate the functioning research-data portal role. | Strong evidence of the scope criterion. Do not manufacture a dataset from publication metadata. |
| Registry operator | Update FAIRsharing **9313**; check its mandatory fields and recommendations; prepare re3data name/URL/description/contact/data-rights entry and evidence links; submit, answer editor questions, then record public IDs. | Submission is external publication; this research did not perform it. Do not make publication depend on optional DOI/certification projects. |

**Submission decision:** apply once the four substantive re3data criteria are publicly evidenced and the form's required information is truthful. The proposed fuller policy suite is useful for stewardship and FAIRsharing recommendations, but the university need not wait for every optional technical feature or certification. Acceptance remains an editorial determination.

## Research limitations and provenance

- Official primary sources were used for registry rules; the live re3data form and hidden FAIRsharing status were inspected with the installed agent-browser skill.
- UIST homepage and current policy text are public observations, not proof of institutional ratification, actual hosting location, operational preservation guarantees, or every item licence.
- FAIRsharing 9313's detailed metadata are hidden while in review. Its warnings must be checked in the authorised maintainer session before recording exact field completion.
- Search did not identify a public re3data UIST record; that establishes no verified public match in this research, not proof that no draft/submission exists.
- Local EDEN documents were read for context. Current user statements supersede their stale Handle/development approval questions.
- The SWISSUbase EDEN assessment URL supplied by the user evaluates SWISSUbase; it cannot establish UIST research-data holdings or acceptance.
