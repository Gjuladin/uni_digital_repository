## Optional information to complete when verified

- Legal institution name and country: UIST; North Macedonia. Confirm responsible office and relation.
- Subjects and content types: use actual approved coverage and registry vocabulary; distinguish datasets from publications, theses and software.
- Software: DSpace; confirm deployed version before entering it.
- Interfaces: documented REST and OAI-PMH, plus other genuinely deployed interfaces. The public OAI-PMH URL in project records is `https://repository.uist.edu.mk/server/oai/request`; recheck before submission.
- PID systems: Handle prefix `20.500.15029` is registered and live, matching the local configuration. On 2026-10-06, [20.500.15029/261](https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.15029/261) resolved globally to the correct public item; see [dated evidence](../HANDLE_ACTIVATION.md). This establishes sample-level operation; new-item minting, full migration/redirect coverage and restart resilience were not tested. Publisher DOIs stored in metadata are not UIST DOI minting.
- Metadata licence: the accepted policy uses CC0 1.0 for public descriptive metadata, subject to its stated privacy and third-party-rights limits. Verify the public statement and exports; CC0 does not apply to all deposited files.
- Policies and preservation: provide current adopted statements and operational limits. Do not enter proposed funded retention or planned fixity checks as active.
- Certification: no certification is evidenced by this task. Registry membership and EDEN discovery success are not certifications.
