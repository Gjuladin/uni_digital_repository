## Shortness and structure

The official policy is unusually compact and uses five labelled blocks. The useful structure for UIST is: metadata reuse; full-object/data rights; accepted content and language/version labels; who may deposit and what review checks occur; preservation, withdrawal and continuity. The data-storage note is a separate short operational page rather than a long legal policy.

## Metadata versus files/licensing

- **Metadata:** non-profit reuse is allowed with source and OAI-record attribution. The policy does not make the metadata rule a blanket licence for deposited files.
- **Files/full data:** rights are object-specific. Reuse generally requires author/title/bibliographic attribution, original record URL, original copyright and licence statement, and unchanged content. Some records carry different conditions. Re3data records CC0/CC at the repository/data level; that does not replace the deposited object’s licence.

## Deposit, access, content and languages

DiRROS accepts all types of materials, including preprints, accepted manuscripts and published versions, with version/date, peer-review and publication-status tags. Depositors are accredited members, academic staff, registered students or employees; authors submit their own work. Administrators check depositor eligibility, scope, format and authenticity, while publisher/funder embargoes delay public visibility. The policy names English, Slovenian, German, Croatian and Hungarian as principal content languages; re3data lists Slovene and English as repository languages. Re3data’s data-access categories show a deliberately mixed model: open by default where possible, with registration, embargo, closed and restricted variants.

## Useful practice for UIST, with boundaries

The strongest transferable practice is the separation of metadata rights from file rights, a small deposit-review checklist, explicit embargo handling, a plain-text README requirement for datasets, and a compact preservation section (backups, original bitstream retention, version/withdrawal rules and continuity transfer). UIST can adapt these as local policy patterns under Pavel Taskov’s rectorate-appointed manager role, with Macedonian and English publication support and UIST’s CC0 metadata decision.

Do not copy DiRROS’s Slovenian funding/statutory references, 20-year retention promise, CTK membership/paid-service model, OAI endpoint, or any foreign governance arrangement. Those are facts about the comparator, not UIST commitments.
