# Balkan references and UIST re3data preparation

Reviewed 6 October 2026. DABAR is the primary design and policy reference. DAIS and DKUM form the rest of the main reference set; DiRROS and InTOR provide supporting examples. These findings inform the short UIST policy pack accepted by the rector (user confirmation received 6 October 2026) rather than importing another institution's rules.

The registry languages describe the repository/data service; the live policy's principal content languages can be broader, especially in DKUM and DiRROS. A CC0 entry in a registry's data-licence list does not establish that all metadata or files use CC0. Consult the public policy and each item. Registry listings also do not establish UIST's capabilities or current certification.

## UIST policy decisions reflected in the rework

UIST is the institutional operator and the rectorate designates the repository administrator. The current administrator's details appear on the Contact page; other public pages refer to the role. Principal publication languages are Macedonian and English. Deposit eligibility is institutional, with no external partner programme. Public descriptive metadata uses CC0; files retain their item-specific rights. The university privacy PDF remains a linked institutional source, supplemented for the repository. Retention and continuity stay proportionate to UIST's service, without an external successor promise.

The repository administrator authorises accounts and collection deposit permissions; authorised UIST users may deposit and publish without individual administrator approval. Depositors are responsible for content, metadata, rights and access conditions. The administrator handles technical support and corrections or restrictions when a problem arises. Comparator policies describe their own administrator checks; those checks are not imported as a required UIST acceptance step. Re3data's published registration criteria do not prescribe approval of every deposit; the authorised self-deposit model is an interpretation consistent with those criteria, not a guarantee of registration.

## Short re3data worksheet

The [current re3data FAQ](https://www.re3data.org/faq) calls for clearly explained repository/data access and terms of use, and an actual research-data focus. It does not prescribe a large multi-document policy packet. The short pages can provide policy evidence once published; rector acceptance was reported on 6 October 2026; listing remains an editorial decision.

| Field or evidence | Proposed UIST treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Name and URL | UIST Digital Repository; https://repository.uist.edu.mk/ |
| Responsible institution | University of Information Science and Technology St Paul the Apostle, North Macedonia; repository administrator details on the Contact page |
| Policy and terms URL | Proposed `/info/repository-policies`; publish first and then supply the real HTTPS URL |
| Catalogue access | Open public catalogue; verify anonymous access at submission |
| Data access | Report access categories present in actual datasets; policy permits open access with justified restrictions or embargoes |
| Upload | Restricted to authorised UIST depositors; institutional membership; authorised users publish without individual administrator approval |
| Metadata/database licence | CC0 1.0 for public descriptive metadata; confirm published statement and exports are consistent |
| Data licence name and URL | Use actual dataset licences and their URLs; consolidated policy explains item-specific rights. Do not substitute metadata CC0 for file rights |
| Languages | Macedonian and English publication scope; report UI/data languages accurately |
| Research data evidence | Link an actual published, documented dataset or an operated research-data service. The local dataset example is not a production holding |
| Technical fields | DSpace; add current version, interfaces, globally resolving identifiers and other fields only as verified |
| Submitter | Use an institution-authorised contact; this task did not submit a suggestion |

Prepare a brief description of actual deposited research data and the account-authorisation process, supported by a representative public record. Describe metadata as depositor-supplied with technical corrections when needed; do not claim routine manual review before publication. Publications and theses can coexist with datasets, as the comparators show; their presence does not establish research-data holdings by itself. UIST Handle prefix `20.500.15029` is live, with global sample `/261` resolution verified on 6 October 2026; cite [dated evidence](../../HANDLE_ACTIVATION.md) in PID fields. Allocation alone, a software default or a comparator's certificate should not become an unsupported field value.

The rector has accepted the short text. Before application, verify implementation of the accepted privacy wording and operating commitments, publish the four pages and retain genuine public dataset evidence. Operational details such as backup frequency belong in internal procedures. See the five source notes in `research/` for the exact pages consulted and distinctions between live policy and registry metadata.
