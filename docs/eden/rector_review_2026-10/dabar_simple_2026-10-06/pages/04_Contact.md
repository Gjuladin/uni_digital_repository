# UIST Repository Contact

The current repository administrator authorised by the rectorate is Pavel Taskov. For account permissions, deposit enquiries, technical corrections and accessibility requests, email [pavel.taskov@uist.edu.mk](mailto:pavel.taskov@uist.edu.mk). Include the relevant record or page URL when available.
